The German government is warning politicians and public figures that they were targeted by government-backed spyware, days after Apple began sending out notifications warning victims that their iPhones may have been hacked Germany’s federal cybersecurity office, known as the BSI, said in a note over the weekend that it had become aware that members of the Bundestag, Germany’s parliament, and other unnamed notable individuals, had received warnings from Apple that they may have been targeted by cyberattacks Apple periodically sends out so-called threat notifications to people it thinks may have been targeted by spyware, which can steal messages, location data, and other sensitive information from their phones This spyware often relies on zero-click exploits , which can hack individuals almost invisibly as they don’t require a victim to click on a link or open a file For that reason, the BSI said that anyone who receives an Apple threat notification “should therefore assume that their device may have been compromised.” Others outside of Germany also received the recent threat alert from Apple Radwan Mortada, a Beirut-based filmmaker, said in a post on X that he received a new threat notification warning that Apple had detected “another attack against your device,” after an earlier spyware attack targeted him in 2021 It’s unclear who is behind this latest round of spyware attacks, or which government may have orchestrated the targeting
