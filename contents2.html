Android app developers may be unwittingly sharing their users’ location data with advertisers

요약

The Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) has revealed that Android app developers may be unknowingly sharing users' precise location data with third-party advertisers and data brokers. This occurs because certain third-party software development kits (SDKs) embedded in apps inherit the app's location permissions by default, collecting user data unless explicitly disabled by the developer. The EFF warns that this data, often monetized by data brokers, poses significant security and privacy risks, as it can be sold to government agencies and is vulnerable to breaches. The organization urges developers to disable unnecessary data collection to protect user privacy.
